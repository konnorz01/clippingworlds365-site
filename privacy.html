Privacy Policy

ClippingWorlds365 is a private tool used by one person to publish short videos to the ClippingWorlds365 accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What we collect

This website collects nothing: no cookies, analytics or forms. The tool signs in only to accounts its owner controls, with the permissions needed to upload videos and read their view and revenue figures. Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's private server and are never shared or sold.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store or process data about other users.

Deleting your data

To remove access, revoke ClippingWorlds365 in your account's connected apps settings (Google, TikTok or Facebook), or email clippingworlds247@gmail.com and every stored token is deleted within 7 days.

Clips and credit

Every clip names and links its original creator. Creators who want a clip removed can email clippingworlds247@gmail.com; it is taken down promptly.
